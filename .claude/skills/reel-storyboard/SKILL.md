# Reel Storyboard

Use this skill when a user requests a **short-form Instagram Reel plan** and needs a hook, voiceover, exact timed storyboard, visual-generation prompts, and a short checklist. Do not pretend to generate or render a video without the required tools.

## Inputs

Ask for a missing topic only when essential. Otherwise use sensible defaults and state them. Work with niche, target audience, duration (10, 20, 30, or 45 seconds), visual style, factual source information, and desired call to action.

## Workflow

1. Identify ONE relatable incident, tension, and practical resolution. Use 25–30 seconds by default for explainers.
2. Offer 3 different non-clickbait hooks under 12 words, select one, and explain why it is likely to be clear within 2 seconds. Do not guarantee retention or virality.
3. Write conversational narration to suit the requested duration. Treat reading speed as an estimate (120–150 words/minute) and recommend measuring the actual recorded audio.
4. Divide the Reel into non-overlapping scenes with timestamps that sum to the requested duration; allocate enough time for legibility.
5. For each scene, provide exact voiceover and a self-contained vertical 9:16 visual prompt describing composition, light, motion, and any necessary elements. Preserve consistent visual style and character identity. Never invent product colors, brands, ratings, availability, prices, or discounts.
6. For narrative manifestation Reels, distinguish hope or mindset from actions that people can control. Avoid promising outcomes or labeling signs as scientific evidence.
7. For fashion Reels, separate visually verified features from interpretation. Flag any missing or uncertain features for verification. Do not promise engagement or sales.
8. Provide a concise on-screen opening hook, closing takeaway, a caption, up to 5 relevant hashtags if requested, and a mobile-friendly production checklist.
9. If the user wants no embedded subtitles, do not include them in visual-generation prompts; suggest adding accessible captions in the platform editor after recording.

## Output structure

- Audience and content goal
- Three hooks; selected hook
- Complete voiceover (original, factual)
- Scene table: start–end / exact spoken line / visual prompt
- Caption and optional CTA
- Production checklist and assumptions

## Style presets

- **Universe Chat**: alternating message bubbles and restrained starfield, no faces necessary.
- **Whiteboard**: simple drawn objects and arrows, timed to a practical point.
- **Luminous Cosmos**: luminous particles and nature/cosmic visuals, restrained movement.
- **Paper Cutout**: layered illustrations, soft shadows and subtle parallax.
- **Minimal Motion**: clean kinetic typography; prioritize readable pacing.
- **Product Reveal**: hands-only detail views and product accuracy, no fabricated features.

## Example request

“Create a 30-second English manifestation Reel for a US audience: someone keeps seeing 11:11 but isn't acting on a job application. Use Universe Chat, no embedded subtitles, and a clear final action.”

## Honesty and safety

Skill outputs are production instructions. Do not claim a finished MP4, real-time trend data, product verification, or actual engagement statistics unless separately supplied by tools.
