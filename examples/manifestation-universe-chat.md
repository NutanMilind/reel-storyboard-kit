
# Manifestation Reel Example — Universe Chat

## Reel Information

- Title: The 11:11 Reminder
- Niche: Manifestation and Mindset
- Audience: English-speaking US audience
- Duration: 30 seconds
- Format: 9:16 vertical (1080x1920)
- Style: Animated Universe Chat
- Voices: Human and Universe
- Subtitles: Add later in Instagram
- Music: Optional, added during editing

## Concept

Someone keeps seeing 11:11 but is not taking action toward getting a new job.

The Universe reminds them that hoping for change is not a substitute for taking practical steps.

## Scene 1 — Opening Hook (0–5s)

Human:
"I keep seeing 11:11. Is that a sign?"

Visual Prompt:
Dark purple starfield. A minimal messaging interface appears. The Human's message bubble slides into view. Subtle golden particles drift in the background.

## Scene 2 — The Question (5–11s)

Universe:
"Maybe. But what goal are you avoiding?"

Visual Prompt:
A warm golden message bubble appears with a gentle glow. The background remains consistent. Use natural message timing and restrained animation.

## Scene 3 — The Real Problem (11–17s)

Human:
"I want a new job. I haven't applied."

Visual Prompt:
A new Human message appears. A small resume icon fades in beside the chat. The mood becomes thoughtful.

## Scene 4 — Practical Advice (17–24s)

Universe:
"Then choose one role. Update your resume and apply today."

Visual Prompt:
A softly glowing Universe message appears. A checklist icon animates with three simple steps: Choose, Update, Apply. Keep the layout readable.

## Scene 5 — Final Takeaway (24–30s)

Universe:
"A sign doesn't send applications. You do. Start today."

Visual Prompt:
The final golden message settles on screen. Subtle cosmic particles move behind it. End with a gentle fade.

## Instagram Caption

Sometimes we keep waiting for the perfect sign, the perfect moment, or the perfect opportunity.

But progress begins when we take one small step toward what matters.

What is one action you can take today?

## Production Checklist

- Record two distinct English voices.
- Use consistent Human and Universe message styles.
- Keep all graphics in a 9:16 safe area.
- Use smooth transitions and readable message bubbles.
- Do not add burned-in narration subtitles.
- Add accessible captions in Instagram Edits.
- Check the final audio timing before publishing.

## Disclaimer

This example is a storyboard and production guide, not a finished MP4 video. It makes no claim that seeing 11:11 predicts future outcomes.
